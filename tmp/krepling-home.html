Frequently Asked Questions

  • Krepling is an all-in-one centralized commerce platform that allows you to turn your idea into any type of online business. Across the globe, entrepreneurs and businesses of all sizes use Krepling to build, grow, and manage their business online. From retail to freelancing, to subscriptions and events, to even digital products and educational courses/programs, Krepling has you covered.

  • Yes, you can use your existing domain name with Krepling. Simply link it from your store's admin. If you're still in need of a domain name, you can purchase one through Krepling or a third-party provider, or take advantage of a free domain with a 'mykrepling.com' suffix.

  • Yes, migrating your existing online store to Krepling is possible and streamlined for convenience. Our platform offers migration tools and support to help transfer your product listings, customer data, and past orders securely. We also have dedicated customer service teams who can assist you throughout the process to ensure a smooth transition.

  • Krepling is designed to help you sell products globally. With our integrated payment gateways and shipping partners, you can reach customers in various international markets. Our platform supports cross-border transactions, and we provide resources to help you comply with local regulations and tax laws.

  • Krepling is built to support multiple languages, enabling you to reach a wider audience. Our platform allows you to create a multi-lingual storefront so you can connect with customers in their native language, providing a more personalized shopping experience. Language support is continually expanding, and we aim to cater to as many languages as possible.
